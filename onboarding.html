Demonstration guide. This fictional application and workflow illustrate my approach to customer onboarding. They do not describe Sciemo's interface.

Your first-session goal

By the end of this session, you should be able to open the correct workspace, find the data relevant to your team, and explain the scope of the view you are using.

Set aside 30 minutes with your onboarding contact. Bring the workspace name, the business question you want to answer, and an example total your team already knows.

Before the session

Your organization should identify a workspace owner and arrange the approved sign-in method. The owner confirms your role and which data you may access. For this example, a Viewer can read authorized data and a Planner can also save planning changes. An administrator assigns those roles.

Use your organization's approved identity provider when single sign-on is configured. Complete MFA through the approved method. Support can help investigate an error; passwords, MFA codes, and recovery codes should remain with you.

1. Open the correct workspace

  1. Follow your approved invitation or workspace link.
  2. Sign in with the work account associated with that invitation.
  3. Confirm the organization and workspace name before opening a report.
  4. If access fails, capture the error wording and time, then use the sign-in guide or workspace access guide.

Check: Tell the onboarding contact which workspace you opened and what your role allows you to do.

2. Establish the scope before reading a number

In this scenario, the overview has a date range, retailer, product group, and scenario selector. Choose those values before comparing a total with another source.

Record the selected values and the latest completed refresh time. A saved view may have different filters from the view a colleague is using.

Check: Explain the business question in one sentence: “I am reviewing October units for Retailer A and Product Group B in the baseline scenario.”

3. Compare one familiar result

Choose a small, known slice of data and compare it with your approved reference.

If the figures differ, check the date boundaries, units, refresh time, and filters first. Record both values and the exact scope. Use the data discrepancy guide if the difference remains unexplained.

Check: You can identify what the number measures and which records it includes.

4. Save or share an authorized view

A Planner may save a view using a name that explains its purpose and period. A Viewer can ask the workspace owner about the approved way to share the same scope.

Sharing a link does not grant someone permission to see the data. Ask the owner to arrange access if needed.

5. Practice without prompts

Open a different authorized product group, state the active filters, and locate its refresh information. Then return to your original view.

Your onboarding contact records which steps you completed independently and which need another walkthrough. Revisit any uncertain step during office hours.

Leave with a next step

Before ending, agree on one task you will complete in the application, its owner, and a check-in date. Ask questions using the workspace name, your goal, and the point where you became stuck.

Success for this session means you completed the practice task and can explain the scope of the result. No adoption or performance improvement is claimed from this demonstration.
